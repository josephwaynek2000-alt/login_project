Dear Iva, I don't know any other way to start this other than I love you. You have helped me grow in numerous ways that I didn't even know needed growth. Thank you for your patience over the past four and a half years. You have always been so caring and supporting of my problems even when you had every reason to get rid of me. I couldn't have asked for a better person to marry me or mother our children. I know life has been kicking your butt lately and I just wanted to say that I am here for you. I may be too much at times, I may even be the problem at times. But I am always here for you. I see your stress, your pain, and your conflict. I see YOU honey. Yet through all of it I still see that strong-willed resilient woman that I fell for. You have time and time again shown that you won;t be beaten by life. That even in the rough times you will pull through. It won't always be an easy, cut and dry win but you'll always get there. You've battled hardship your entire life in all forms. Mentally you've been knocked down by the words and actions of others (unfortunately myself included). Physically you have both been hurt by other people as well as your own mental turmoil. Spiritually, you are handling a conflict that modern culture has just decided to pretend isn't a conflict at all. You are the epitome of strength. Through all of these things you have loved like no other. You're raising our children in a way that we both wanted to be raised. You are growing mentally everyday and learning to overcome your physical hurdles in a way that isn't controlled by a clouded mental state. I know right now is a very confusing time in our lives, both with me being deployed and with you discovering yourself. All I can say is, your biggest fan is right behind you every step of the way. I would love for us to be able to figure this out and be the husband and wife that shows everyone this is possible. I'd love to be the best support system I can be as I have been called to do by our vows. I won't ever give up on you, just as I pray you never give up on me. Without you I wouldn't be who I am today. I would've just kept living life lost in the misconceptions of the world. My maturity and the person writing this letter is because of you. You taught me where I was wrong, you taught me how to fix it, and you not only waited me out but you held me the whole way through until I became what you needed me to be. Which in hand made us good for each other. That alone deserves the universe. Thank you for everything honey. YOU ARE PERFECT. I love you more than any letter or words could ever explain, Joseph King (Your grateful bee)
